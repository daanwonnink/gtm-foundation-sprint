Before execution

Three questions that
need answers first.

These are the diagnostic questions the sprint answers. If you can't answer them confidently today, your GTM motion is built on assumptions.

01 — ICP
Who is your highest-probability buyer right now — and is the market large enough to get you where you want to go?
Not a persona. A precise profile specific enough that two people independently identify the same company.
02 — Motion
What GTM motion fits your product, your market, and what your team can actually sustain for 2+ years?
The motion has to match your ACV, product complexity, time to value, and team capacity — not just what's trending.
03 — Priorities
Of all the opportunities in front of you — which align with where you are now, and which are distractions?
